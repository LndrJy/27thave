# 27th Marketing: Services page opener (TREATMENT)

Style: Dark Tech Keynote (library `dark-keynote`). Format: 3840x2160, 24 fps, about 35 s, text only, music + foley, English.
Plays muted-first on https://27thmarketing.com/Services, so every idea is readable without sound; sound is a bonus for people who unmute.

## Brand inputs (from the site and both socials)
- Stage: near-black `#08080A` (the site's own theme color).
- Logo: navy disc, golden "27TH" with a rising arrow, "MARKETING" beneath. Accent = that gold (final hex sampled from the logo file; working value `#F2C230`). Navy only as a faint off-centre glow on the stage, never as a fill.
- Voice of the copy is the site's: short, blunt, no hard sell. All headline lines are taken from the Services page.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Zoom ladder.** One pixel, one clip, one site, one system. Elegant, but it explains scale, not what the agency is.
2. **Service countdown.** Four services ticking by like a release checklist. Clear, but it is a list, and a list is what every agency video is.
3. **Five into one.** Five disconnected freelancer tiles drift and clash (the pain), everything freezes, then snaps into a single grid (the agency). The four services then each open from one grid tile, the process runs as a line, one number proves it, and the grid collapses into the logo.

**Chosen: 3.** It states the agency's actual pitch ("No more juggling five freelancers", "One team, one strategy"), it matches the Instagram post "Most brands duct-tape 5 freelancers together", and the style's native move (the snap grid) is the idea, not decoration.

## Logline and arc
Five separate freelancers become one team, and the one team does everything behind the video.
Setup: scattered, mismatched tiles (problem). Turn: freeze, silence, snap into one grid. Ending: grid collapses into the logo and one call to action.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Learn: Apple keynote product reveals: black stage, one line per screen, generous negative space, light sweeps, slow confident push-ins, one giant number. Don't take: any Apple type, UI, melody, or product shapes. Reference video the client shared is for feel (minimal, smooth, cinematic), not for copying shots.

## Cue map and sound
- Bars of 2.5 s; every cut and snap lands on a beat, moves start one frame early.
- Problem section: marimba and glockenspiel, detuned tens of cents, random pans (scattered family).
- Freeze (shot 2): true digital silence, 2 beats. First sound after it is the snap: felt mallet + sine drop, then one in-key chord.
- Service section: one vibraphone/celesta family, one note per tile (additive build), felt ticks and glass taps, all centred.
- Process: phasing pattern, a low sine pulse under the number.
- Ending: notes removed one per beat until a single celesta note rings on the logo.
- Silences: shot 2, plus one half-beat before "3.5M". J-cut: the snap chord rings into shot 4.
- Mix: -14 LUFS. Instruments from the library's CC0 sample packs, or synthesized in code.

## Type
Inter (headlines, 500/600), Inter Tight 600 for the giant numbers, JetBrains Mono for labels. One line per screen. Headlines are on-stage type (not a bottom toast) because there is no voice. Hold = letters/15 + 1.5 s minimum, checked with `readcheck`.
